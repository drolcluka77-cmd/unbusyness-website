What Unbusyness does

An office manager that knows every building.

It works with the contractors and suppliers you already use. Residents don't need a new app: they call, text or email like they do today.

Answers residents 24/7

By phone, SMS or email, in your residents' language. Every report gets the building, flat, problem and photos.

Knows what is an emergency

Water, gas, a person stuck in the lift: the on-call contractor and you are alerted at once. A squeaky door waits for the morning.

Sends and follows up contractors

The right plumber or electrician for that building gets the job with all details. Residents get updates without calling you.

Keeps every date

Inspections, servicing and the cleaning rota per building, with reminders and the contractor booked before the deadline.

Owners' meetings without the paperwork

Invitations, agenda and minutes sent to every owner, and decisions saved with the building.

How it starts

We set it up with you. It takes about a week.

  1. Send us your list

    Buildings, flats, contractors and the dates you track, in any format: Excel, PDF or a photo of your notebook.

  2. Residents get one contact

    One phone number and email for all your buildings, or keep yours and forward it. We prepare the notice for the noticeboard.

  3. You stop being the switchboard

    Reports, jobs and dates arrive sorted. You decide what needs you, from your phone or computer.

Questions

What managers ask us first

Do residents need to install an app?

No. They call, send an SMS or email. If they want, they can follow their report through a link.

Can I keep my contractors?

Yes. You add the plumbers, electricians, lift and cleaning companies you already work with, per building.

Does it speak Slovenian?

Yes, Slovenian and English to start, with other languages of the region next.

What will it cost?

It is free during the pilot. After that, pricing depends on the number of buildings, and pilot managers keep founding terms.

What happens with residents' data?

It stays yours, is stored in the EU and is only used to run your buildings. You can export or delete it at any time.

Who is behind Unbusyness?

A small Slovenian team that runs service businesses itself and builds the software it wished it had.
