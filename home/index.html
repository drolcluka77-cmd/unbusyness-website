Property passport

A home with a history sells differently.

Every repair, service and document is saved to your property's passport as it happens. When you sell or rent out, you don't promise that the home was looked after. You show it.

✓

What Unbusyness Home does

Organises your home, and connects you with people who turn up.

A passport for everything

Home, car, appliances, warranties, invoices and manuals. A photo is enough, nothing to type.

Reminders before it's due

Services, registrations, warranties and filter changes. You're reminded in time, with a tradesperson ready to book if you need one.

Report with one message

A photo, a text or a voice note is enough. Unbusyness works out what is broken and who should fix it.

Reliable, punctual tradespeople

Verified plumbers, electricians and other trades, rated on whether they arrive on time and do the job right. You get a fixed arrival window.

Every repair on record

Invoices, photos and new warranties are saved automatically. That history is useful when you sell, rent out or claim on insurance.

Shared with your household

Your partner sees the same passport. A tenant can report faults without having your phone number for everything.

Living in an apartment building?

Faults in shared areas go straight to your building manager.

When your building manager uses Unbusyness, a broken lift, a dark stairwell or a leaking roof is reported with one message, and you can follow the repair until it's done.

Questions

What residents ask us first

Does my building manager have to use Unbusyness?

No. The passport, reminders and tradespeople work on their own. If your building manager uses Unbusyness too, you can also report faults in shared areas and follow the repairs.

How do you choose tradespeople?

We work with verified tradespeople and track whether they arrive within the agreed window and how residents rate the work. Those who keep missing appointments don't get new jobs.

What will it cost?

We're still deciding on pricing. Everyone on the waitlist gets early access and founding terms.

Who sees my data?

Only you and the people you share your passport with. A tradesperson sees only what is needed for the job, such as the address and the photo of the fault.

I'm renting. Is it for me?

Yes. You can report faults to your landlord or building manager and keep a record of what was reported and when.
