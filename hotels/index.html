What Unbusyness does

Nothing new for staff to learn.

Your team keeps using WhatsApp. Unbusyness reads each message, creates the job and makes sure it gets done.

Text, voice note or photo

"Room 305, TV not working, and they need towels" becomes two jobs for two departments. Voice notes in any language become clear written tasks.

One owner for every job

The right person on shift gets it, by department, floor and skill, and accepts it with one tap. No more "I thought you had it".

Target times that climb

You set how long each type of job may take to accept and to finish. If a time is missed, the next person up is told.

Night rules

At night, urgent jobs such as a water leak or a broken lock go straight to the duty manager. The rest waits for the morning shift.

Outside contractors too

When the job needs a plumber, lift service or electrician from outside, Unbusyness books them and keeps the room status up to date.

Facts for decisions

Time to accept and to fix by department, floor and request type, and across all your hotels, with a short summary on WhatsApp.

If a job runs late

Only the late ones reach you.

Managers aren't copied on every message. They are told when a target time is missed, so attention goes where it is needed.

Questions

What hotels ask us first

Do staff need a new app?

No. They report on WhatsApp by text, voice note or photo. Managers also get a web overview.

Which languages does it understand?

Slovenian, English, German and Italian to start. Voice notes in many more languages are turned into clear written tasks.

Who decides the rules?

You do: departments, target times and who is alerted when. The same situation always gives the same result, and selected actions can wait for a manager's approval.

What will it cost?

It is free during the pilot. After that you pay a monthly price per hotel that depends on the number of rooms, and pilot hotels keep founding terms.

What about guests' and staff data?

It is stored in the EU, each hotel's data is kept separate, and we sign a GDPR data-processing agreement with you.

Does it work for hotel groups?

Yes. Each hotel has its own setup, and the owner sees one overview across all properties.
